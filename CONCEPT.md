# REED - The AI Business Co-Pilot: Concept & Vision

## 1. Vision Statement

To evolve REED from a specialized market research tool into a comprehensive, AI-powered "Business Co-Pilot". This platform will assist entrepreneurs and business owners at every stage of their journey, from initial ideation and research to daily operations and strategic growth.

## 2. Core Principles

*   **Context-Aware AI Interaction:** The chat and input bar serve as the primary user interface. The AI must always be aware of the user's current context—which section they are in (e.g., Research, Work), what specific data they are viewing (a report, a company card), and the history of their interactions. This ensures relevant, intelligent, and proactive assistance.
*   **Modular & Integrated Toolset:** The platform is built as a series of modular sections (like "Research" and "Work"). While each module serves a specific purpose, they are interconnected. Insights from market research can seamlessly flow into planning within a company workspace, and operational data can inform new research queries.
*   **Action-Oriented Output:** The AI's goal is not just to provide information but to generate actionable outputs, such as business plans, strategic recommendations, task lists, and content, which can be directly utilized or exported.

## 3. Development Roadmap

This document outlines a phased approach to development, allowing for iterative feedback and refinement.

### Phase 1: Market Research & Idea Validation (Completed)

*   **Functionality:**
    *   **Explore Ideas:** Generate a list of potential business ideas within a broad topic.
    *   **Analyze Niche:** Conduct a deep-dive market analysis on a specific business niche, providing data on market size, trends, audience, competitors, and a SWOT analysis.
    *   **Business Plan Generation:** Create a lean, actionable business plan from a selected idea.
    *   **Contextual Follow-up Chat:** Allow users to ask detailed follow-up questions about the generated reports.
*   **Status:** Implemented.

### Phase 2: The Project Workspace (Completed)

*   **Goal:** To provide tools for users who have an existing business, project, or validated idea.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Dual-Section Layout:** Introduced a main navigation splitting the app into "Research" (existing tools) and "Work" (new workspace).
    *   **Company Cards:** A central hub for managing individual projects, businesses, or ideas.
    *   **Project Workspace View:** A detailed view for each project featuring a two-panel layout.
    *   **Advanced File Manager:** Implemented a full-featured file manager with support for creating folders, renaming assets, and organizing files via drag-and-drop.
    *   **Document Management:** Users can upload files, create new text documents directly in the app, and view PDFs and images.
    *   **Contextual Multi-File Chat:** Implemented drag-and-drop of multiple files into the chat bar, which are displayed in the chat history. The AI uses all attached files as the primary context for its answers.
*   **Status:** Implemented.

### Phase 3: Deep AI Integration & Workflow Automation (Next Step)

*   **Goal:** To transform the workspace from a data repository into an interactive, intelligent environment for analysis and creation.
*   **Potential Features:**
    *   **Direct Document Actions:** AI-powered buttons directly in the Document Viewer (e.g., "Summarize this document," "Extract key takeaways," "Identify action items").
    *   **Cross-Document Analysis:** Allow users to select multiple documents in the File Explorer and ask comparative or synthetic questions (e.g., "Compare the Q2 and Q3 financial reports," "Synthesize a marketing plan from these three documents").
    *   **Enhanced Document Creation:** Upgrade the text editor to a rich-text (Markdown) editor. Introduce AI templates for creating common business documents (e.g., meeting agendas, project proposals, marketing briefs).
    *   **Initial Integrations:** Implement the "Link from Google Drive" functionality.

### Phase 4: Financial & Operational Tooling (Future)

*   **Goal:** To integrate tools for financial planning and day-to-day operations.
*   **Potential Features:**
    *   AI-assisted financial modeling (e.g., "Build a 3-year forecast based on this business plan").
    *   Marketing content generation (social media posts, blog outlines, email campaigns).
    *   KPI tracking and performance analysis dashboards.

### Phase 5: Collaboration & Integration (Future)

*   **Goal:** To enable team collaboration and connect REED with other business tools.
*   **Potential Features:**
    *   Multi-user workspaces.
    *   Task assignment and project management features.
    *   Deeper integrations with platforms like Notion, Slack, Google Workspace, etc.
